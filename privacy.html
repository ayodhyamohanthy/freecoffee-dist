PRIVACY POLICY

What we store.

Last updated October 16, 2026. FreeCoffee is built in public and run by one person. This page says what the site, the sign-in and the Mac app handle. Questions: hello@freecoffee.tech.

SIGN-IN

If you sign in with GitHub or Google.

What we keep

Your provider's account id, your display name, a link to your profile picture, the date the account was created, and a hashed session token. That is all.

What we do not keep

We do not store your email address, your password, or any access token from GitHub or Google. We ask the provider only for your basic profile (GitHub: read:user, Google: openid and profile). We never read your repositories, mail, contacts or files.

Your session

After you sign in, a random session token is saved in your browser (local storage on freecoffee.tech). We store only a hash of it. Signing out deletes the stored session. Clearing your browser data also signs you out.

Deleting your account

Write to hello@freecoffee.tech from a message that names your provider and display name, and we will delete the account record.

THE MAC APP AND ADS

What the ad system handles.

What is collected

An install pseudonym, the campaign and slot that were shown, and whether the placement was displayed. It does not read your screen, files, app names, browser history, window titles or keystrokes.

Targeting

Only the categories you chose and a coarse region. It is not used to track you across other companies' apps or sites.

FORMS

If you write to us.

Sponsor and pilot forms

If you submit an advertiser or pilot form, we store what you type (company, name, contact details, creative) so we can review and reply. We use it only for that review.

HOSTING

Who handles the data.

Services we use

The site is served from GitHub Pages. Accounts, sessions and the ad ledger run on Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare storage. These providers process standard connection data such as IP addresses to deliver pages. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with advertisers. Advertisers see only aggregate delivery counts.

Changes

If this changes in a way that affects you, we will update this page and its date.
