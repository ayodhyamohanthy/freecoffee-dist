Referrals

You earn 10% of your referee's base earnings for 12 months — gated on them logging ≥60 verified minutes in their first 30 days. Demo data, seeded (seed 42).

Your referral link

Anyone who installs FreeCoffee through this link counts as your referee — the demo's two referees arrived exactly this way.

X WhatsApp LinkedIn

Link mechanics: the code is generated in your browser and is not your device id — the device id is the only identifier the platform holds, so a share link must never carry it. Anyone arriving through this link is bound to you server-side, and their 10% is held until they log 60 verified minutes inside 30 days. Release, term and voiding are computed in backend/src/worker.js — not here. Every platform artifact for this same code lives on the flywheel page.

Demo referees below (seeded ledger): astra.dev (verified, 74 minutes) and quill@mac (41 of 60 gate minutes). The real numbers live in the line above, and they only appear once a backend URL is configured.

Rewards

10% of referee base earnings, 12 months. Accrues daily against their simulated flights, straight into your ledger as referral rows.

The gate: ≥60 verified minutes in their first 30 days. No gate-passing, no payout — which keeps the program worth funding and referral spam worthless.

Referral earnings come out of the platform side, never out of your referee's balance. See the payout model for the sustainability math.
