THE FEED CONTRACT

One URL. No rebuild.

A feed is any HTTPS URL returning a JSON array of campaigns. Host the JSON anywhere — GitHub raw, your CDN, your own server — paste the URL into your profile page, and the campaigns appear in the Mac status area and dashboard.

[ { "id": "camp_001", "sponsor": "Acme Coffee Co.", "tagline": "Single-origin beans — 20% off first bag", "clickUrl": "https://advertiser.example/offer", "cpm": 6.0, "logoText": "AC", "accent": "#C98F4E" } ]
Campaign feed field reference
FieldTypeRules
idstringUnique per campaign, max 40 chars. Required when publishing through the backend — the id is the earnings pay-key, and duplicates are dropped.
sponsorstringBrand name, max 40 chars. Required — entries without it are skipped.
taglinestringOne line, max 80 chars.
clickUrlstringMust be https://. Anything else is stripped before the campaign is accepted.
cpmnumberClamped to 0–100. This is the rate a verified minute is paid against.
logoTextstringUp to 2 characters, used for the tile.
accentstringHex colour for the tile.

Advertiser questions.

What does the ad contain?

A logo tile, headline, one-line description, and reviewed destination.

How does bidding work?

You set a CPM on each four-hour block you want. The highest CPM on a block takes every impression in it and pays the bid they set. Ties go to whoever bid first.

What if I lose?

Nothing is charged. You only pay for the blocks you win, at the CPM you bid.

How is delivery verified?

Only eligible, valid display contributes to the signed aggregate receipt.

What happens on underdelivery?

The receipt records it and the frozen policy determines a make-good or refund.

See how your brand fits.

Preview the real unit before you submit. Campaigns sent through the open queue are reviewed by a human before they serve. A winning slot bid enters the serving list directly — so bid on creative you would stand behind in public.
