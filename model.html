The levers

What you get Parameters & caps Why it's built this way
Base share — 70% of auction revenue, always on 70% of auction revenue from eligible impressions. No revenue milestone is required. The base share and promotional bonuses are separate ledger entries so each amount can be checked.
Launch boost — 10% of auction revenue During launch, the platform funds an additional 10 percentage points on top of the 70% base share. A promotional boost, not a permanent increase to the base share. The simulator lets you turn it off.
Payout setup and minimum Setup unlocks at $5 in earnings, with no setup fee. Withdrawals require at least $10 available after five days pending. Pending earnings cannot be withdrawn. Stripe and Whop are simulated choices in this preview.
Referrals — 5% to each person for the first 30 days The referrer receives 5% of the referred user's base earnings; the referred user receives another 5%. Last-click attribution lasts 14 days. Both bonuses are platform-funded, never deducted from the referred user's base. No earnings means no referral bonus.
Snooze on your schedule 30 minutes, 2 hours, 6 hours, or the rest of the day. The demo pauses its sponsor rotation and earning animation, then resumes when the snooze expires.
Transparency — winning bid + your exact cut, every row Every ledger row carries the auction's winning bid, your cut, the type, and the country tier. Your 70% is recomputable from your own data at any time. "Trust us" is not an audit trail. If the share is real, showing the bid costs nothing and proves everything.

How we stay honest

Claims we'll make

  • 70% base share plus a platform-funded 10% launch boost.
  • 5% for the referrer and 5% for the referred user in their first 30 days; 14-day last-click attribution.
  • $10 minimum after a five-day pending period; setup unlocks at $5 in earnings.
  • All balances and withdrawals in this preview are simulated.
  • See the winning bid and your cut on every impression.

Claims we won't make

  • We won't promise guaranteed earnings, permanent launch bonuses, or a live payout-provider connection in this demo.
  • We won't quote competitor numbers we can't verify ourselves, or imply they've changed since we last checked.
  • We won't promise payout rails we don't have — "rails subject to provider availability" is printed right on the payout table below.

Anti-fraud: why the caps exist

Every dollar of fraud is a dollar taken from honest users and honest advertisers. The controls below are the entire list — no mystery heuristics.

Impression verification

An impression counts only while the Mac is awake and unlocked, with a coarse recent-activity signal. Minutes you spend asleep or logged out never bill an advertiser — so they never inflate a balance that then has to be clawed back.

Pending review

Earnings remain pending for five days. Only available funds count toward the $10 withdrawal minimum. This preview uses a fixed demo clock and does not settle real funds.

Referral eligibility

Bonuses apply to base earnings in the referred user's first 30 days. Attribution follows the last eligible click within 14 days. No activity means no base earnings or referral bonus.

90-day clawback

Confirmed fraud (emulators, click scripting, gate-gaming) voids the affected earnings for up to 90 days after they were paid. Honest users never see this; it exists so the honest pool doesn't fund the dishonest one.

Device limits

One earning device per account. A second Mac can run FreeCoffee for convenience, but only the designated earning device accrues impressions — so one person can't be 24 impressions a minute.

What we don't do

No screen capture, no keystroke logging, no file scanning, no window-title hashing — see the data boundary. Fraud controls work on coarse signals and math, not surveillance.
