The short version, in plain words.

These cover the staged launch. A longer legal version will follow before public launch.

Status of the service

FreeCoffee is in an early, staged launch. Some features on this site are previews that use simulated data and are marked as such. Payout rails shown as simulated are not live. These terms are a plain-language summary for the current stage and may change as the service does.

Who may use it

You must be able to enter a binding agreement where you live and be old enough to do so.

Earnings

Earnings are your share of what advertisers actually paid for valid delivered minutes. They are not guaranteed, may be zero, and depend on sponsors, delivery, the auction price and our fraud checks. Earnings can be held, adjusted or reversed if they come from invalid or fraudulent activity.

Payouts

New earnings stay pending for five days. Withdrawals need $10 available. Payouts are made only through a rail that is shown as live. Taxes on what you earn are your responsibility.

Acceptable use

Do not generate fake views or clicks, run the app in a way meant to fool the counting, or interfere with the service. We may suspend an account that does.

Advertisers

Advertisers pay for verified delivery under the ad policy. Bids are first-price: the winner pays its own bid. Your first $100 of media carries no platform fee. We may reject or remove any ad that breaks the ad policy.

Your data

The app does not read your screen, files, apps, browser history, window titles, keystrokes or screenshots. The data we do hold is described in the FAQ.

Ending things

You can stop using the service at any time. We may stop it or change it with reasonable notice. We will tell you what happens to any available balance.

Liability

The service is provided as it is. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for lost earnings or indirect losses. Nothing here limits rights you have by law.
